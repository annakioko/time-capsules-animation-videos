**Description**
A day in the life of a Song dynasty prince: every meal is tasted for poison, and the woman who loves him most is the servant paid to feed him.

It's 969 AD in Kaifeng, capital of the Song Empire. The prince saved by the palace wet nurse is now 9 years old. He bows to the mother who gave birth to him, studies under a tutor who reports to the Emperor, refuses a sweet from the Empress, and whispers a word he's never allowed to say. Then, the true story of a real Song emperor who called the wrong woman "mother" for most of his life.
