At sunrise, you are taken to greet your mother. Your real mother. The consort who gave birth to you, and who has climbed the ranks of the palace because you lived.

You kneel. You touch your forehead to the floor. She asks about your lessons, your health, your manners. She does not hug you. Princes are not hugged in public.

To her, you are not just a son. You are her future. If you become Emperor one day, she becomes the most powerful woman in the empire. If you fail, she is nothing.

Outside the hall, the nurse waits. She is not allowed inside. She is a servant.
