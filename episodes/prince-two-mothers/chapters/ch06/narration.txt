At night, she is the one who stays. She checks the windows. She tastes your water. She sits by your bed until your eyes close.

And tonight, half asleep, you whisper the word you are never allowed to say out loud.

"Goodnight, mother."

She freezes. Then she presses her hand over your mouth, gently, and looks at the door. Because if the consort ever hears you call a servant "mother", the nurse will be sent away. Or worse.

She kisses your forehead. She says nothing. But she does not stop crying until the candle goes out.
