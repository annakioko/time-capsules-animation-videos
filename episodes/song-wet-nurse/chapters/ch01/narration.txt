It is nine sixty A.D. You haven't slept in three days. Your head is pounding, your breasts are engorged, and your hands are shaking so violently you're afraid you'll drop the porcelain bowl in front of you.

Why? Because the Emperor's third concubine is convinced the Empress is trying to murder her newborn son. And in the Inner Court of the Song Dynasty, a jealous wife doesn't target the baby directly. She targets the milk.

Which means before a single drop touches the lips of the infant prince... you have to taste-test it first. You take a breath, staring down at the white liquid. If the Empress succeeded, this is your last meal.
