How did you find yourself in the most dangerous room in the empire? Just three months ago, you were a peasant woman in a muddy village. You had just given birth to your own child. But in the Imperial Court, a woman's milk is a commodity.

When the Emperor's favorite concubine fell pregnant, her eunuchs scoured the provinces looking for women with the perfect "constitution." They inspected your skin, your teeth, and the health of your own baby. You were chosen.

But there was a price. To feed a prince, you had to leave your own child behind in the mud. You were brought through the massive vermilion gates of the palace, stripped, scrubbed, and dressed in silks you aren't even allowed to keep.

You quickly learned the brutal hierarchy of the Harem. The Lady who hired you is a consort, a glorified second-class wife. Her entire existence, her safety, and her wealth depend on the survival of the boy she just birthed. If he lives, she might become an Empress Dowager one day. If he dies, she is nothing. And you? You are just the vessel keeping her ambition alive.
